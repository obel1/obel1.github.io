Alright ladies and gentlemen, this is a pretty good product, for females.

1. it has a few of the standard coverage also offered by other companies ladies' plan such as:
    - death and disability benefit
    - female illness benefit
    - pregnancy complications
    - details for the above attached at the bottom of the post
2. it also has pretty good cash compensation:
    - cash value that accumulates year by year
    - 3% of sum assured for marriage, and/or childbirth
    - if no claim for marriage or childbirth, then you get 12% at age 55
    - also, there's a yearly cash bonus starting at 10%, and increasing by +1% each year based on your Vitality status. it goes up to 50%. even say at 25%, that's incredible savings.
3. following number (2.) above, for example, if you're 23 and you got the 150K sum assured plan, which is about RM 3,500 per year
    - when you get married, 3% is RM 4,500
    - you will also get RM 4,500 per childbirth
    - at age 55, you'll receive 12%, less the payout from marriage or childbirth, if any
4. this plan is the gateway to a guaranteed approval when you apply for a pre-natal baby plan (A-Life Joy Xtra/ ALJX)!!
    - do you know how many declined applications i receive per year for pre-natal plans?? it's very frustrating, to the expectant mother especially
    - this plan takes that worry away
    - of course, there's another cash bonus, on top of (3.). it's another 3%!!
    - so if you have this plan, and apply for ALJX, that's RM 9K when the child is born, based on the 150K sum assured
5. things to note:
    - entry age between 16-45, females only. there is no male counterpart to this plan
    - this plan does not come attached with a medical card, CI or PA coverage. it's not meant as your first or only insurance plan
    - to get the most benefit out of Vitality, you're encouraged to be a physically active person

Cheers! - wan
