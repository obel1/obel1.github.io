If you're insured with AIA, whether it's an individual policy or an employee benefit, you should use MyAIA.

MyAIA is available on the [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/my/app/my-aia/id1286476389), [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.aia.myaia) and also the [web portal](https://www.aia.com.my/en/my-aia/myaia-login.html).

These are some of the features:

**1. Policy information and self-servicing:**

- view your policy details and benefits, including your family members if you're the policy owner
- for policies issued from 2018 onwards, you will also be able to view the e-Policy
- virtual medical card
- Changing contact information like address, phone number and email
- make payment directly to AIA (please disable pop-up blocker)
- changing your payment card (please disable pop-up blocker)
- changing your payment mode, for example, from monthly to yearly
- submit claims online (up to RM 500)
- premium statement for LHDN deductions

**2. General info:**

- view panel clinics and hospitals

**3. AIA Vitality, if you are subscribed:**

- view rewards and points
- link your fitness app and smart watch
- complete health assessments

**4. Doc2us online consultation:**

- [https://youtu.be/ylXua6E6qyo](https://youtu.be/ylXua6E6qyo) (this is a paid service)

**5. Financial health check:**

- you'll get a rough idea on how you could improve on your coverage, if there are recommendations

- as a token of appreciation for reducing my workload
